# product-line-review-notes

Product line review — running notes. Owner: Marcus Webb, Head of Product.
These notes track GROSS BOOKINGS. Finance reports NET revenue. The two diverge.

## Q2 review — mid-market pricing

Decision: defer the mid-market repricing to Q4, pending a churn read on the current
price point.

Rationale at the time: we did not have enough evidence that price was driving churn,
and a repricing during the migration would have confounded the signal. The team agreed
explicitly that the condition for revisiting was a clean churn read.

Owner: Marcus. Revisit trigger: the August churn analysis.

## Q2 review — packaging

Agreed to hold packaging changes until the repricing decision is made. No change this
quarter.

## July bookings

Gross bookings +8.4% MoM. July closed three mid-market deals above $40k ACV, which is
what drives the increase.

Important: this is a bookings number. It is not the same as the net revenue figure
Finance carries in the board deck, and it is not adjusted for the August service credit
or for contract-timing shifts. Quoting +8.4% alongside the board deck's revenue figures
invites a direct contradiction, because the deck reports the new product line down
12.1% MoM on a net basis.

## August pipeline

Pipeline going into Q4 looks healthy on a coverage basis: 3.1x on the new product line.
Coverage is a leading indicator only. Two of the largest opportunities are with accounts
that are already flagged as churn risks in the churn analysis, so the coverage number
is more fragile than it appears.
