# churn-analysis-august

August churn analysis. Owner: Sarah Chen. Status: preliminary — the cohort model is
still processing, so treat cuts below the segment level as directional.

## Headline

Monthly logo churn: 4.1%, up from 3.4% in Q2. Net revenue retention: 97%, down from
104%.

This is the highest monthly churn figure in eight quarters.

## By segment

- Enterprise: 1.2% monthly churn, flat quarter over quarter. No concern.
- Mid-market: 6.8% monthly churn, up from 4.4%. This segment accounts for
  approximately 80% of the total increase.
- SMB: 3.9% monthly churn, up from 3.6%. Within normal variance.

The increase is a mid-market problem, not a general one.

## Stated reasons at cancellation

Of 34 mid-market cancellations with an exit reason recorded:

- Price / value: 19 (56%)
- Missing functionality: 7 (21%)
- Consolidating vendors: 5 (15%)
- Other or unrecorded: 3 (9%)

Price is the leading stated reason by a wide margin. This is the churn read the Q2
product line review named as the condition for reopening the mid-market repricing
decision — the condition has now been met.

## Caveat

Stated exit reasons are self-reported and skew toward price, because price is the
easiest reason to give. The cohort model that would separate genuine price sensitivity
from dissatisfaction is still processing. Anyone claiming the analysis has proven that
price causes the churn is overstating what this document supports.
