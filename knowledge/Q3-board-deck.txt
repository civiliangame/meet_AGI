# Q3-board-deck

Acme Corp — Q3 Board Deck. Prepared by Sarah Chen, VP Finance.
All figures are NET revenue on the accrual basis unless a slide says otherwise.

## Slide 3 — Executive summary [p.3]

Total revenue came in at $11.4M for the quarter, approximately 3% ahead of plan.
Growth is carried almost entirely by the core product line. The new product line is
below plan and declining month over month. Churn is the metric management is most
concerned about heading into Q4.

## Slide 11 — Core product line [p.11]

Core Product Line: $9.98M, +4.2% MoM.
Enterprise renewals were unusually strong in August, with three seven-figure accounts
renewing early. That pull-forward flatters August and will not repeat in Q4.

## Slide 14 — New product line [p.14]

New Product Line: $1.42M (-12.1% MoM).

The new product line declined for the second consecutive month. The decline is
concentrated in mid-market accounts. This is the figure carried into the board summary
on slide 3 and into the full-year reforecast.

Definition note: the figures on this slide are NET revenue — recognised revenue after
credits, refunds, and contract-timing adjustments. Gross bookings for the same line are
tracked separately by the product team and are NOT comparable to this number.

## Slide 16 — Revenue adjustments and credits [p.16]

A $180K service credit was issued in August against a mid-market cohort following the
July availability incident. The credit is netted against the new product line, which
accounts for roughly 4 points of the -12.1% MoM movement on slide 14.

Contract timing moved a further $240K of new-product bookings out of the quarter into
Q4 recognition. Bookings and recognised revenue therefore diverge materially this
quarter, and the two should not be quoted interchangeably.

## Slide 22 — Churn [p.22]

Monthly logo churn rose to 4.1%, up from 3.4% in Q2. The increase is concentrated in
mid-market. Early indications point at pricing rather than product quality, but the
August churn analysis was not complete at the time this deck was prepared.

## Slide 27 — Q4 outlook [p.27]

Plan assumes the new product line stabilises rather than recovers. Any Q4 forecast that
assumes continued growth in the new product line is inconsistent with the trend on
slide 14 and should be challenged.
